View the themes of support requests by status - Already Built, In Progress, or Future Step. Each theme has a summary and comments from the Performance team.

Already Built

Increased Transparency on Program Changes

Leaders and partners need more visibility into why program decisions are made and how employee needs are incorporated.

Where you can find it

Q2FY26 Leader Training: Every slide included a "what we heard" bucket before presenting the update. Q4FY26: Leader training will include direct references to how listening results inform program updates. September: Q4 listening results will be shared back with leaders and PeePs/LC when available.

  • Having more on the 'why' on how program decisions are made in service to the business
  • More clarity to the business in how we are listening to and solving for their needs/asks via the program
  • Ensuring the needs and voice of the business are represented and incorporated
  • Business alignment and clear actions related to talent definitions
  • More advance notice of changes to definitions and processes
  • Need changes to feel different to managers to show progress and that we are listening to front line leaders

Clearer Definitions & Consistent Program Rules

Ambiguity in labels like CTR and NME, inconsistent rule enforcement, and the need for sharper, consistently applied definitions.

Where you can find it

Updated guidance has been provided in Workday and throughout Performance collateral for Critical to Retain, Meeting/Not Meeting Expectations, and Guiding Principles and Leadership Expectations.

  • Need to be clearer with leaders on what the labels like CTR will mean (e.g. stock prioritization)
  • CTR wasn't marked as required and sometimes blank and the 20% isn't forced so requires rework
  • Guidance and more robust definitions of what NME is and is not - for leaders in the Talent Assessment and/or FAQs
  • Clarity of the conversations and NME guidance
  • Not going back when we say no MYE/EOY exceptions
  • Critical to retain helps, but still feel like there's a gap in understanding where people stand with performance and CTR doesn't capture full story

People Partner Role Clarity & Workload

PPs are owning too much of the process and want to operate more as consultants, with better cross-P&C integration and easier access to support.

Where you can find it

Your Leader Consultant is your "phone a friend" for support. The People Guide, P&C Assistant in CIRCUIT, HelpZone articles, and Performance site have all the resources you need. Sending org-specific comms is optional. Performance leader training includes Rewards content, and Rewards leader training includes ties back to Performance — helping P&C show up as one team.

  • Lighter lift for people partners - it feels that we own comms, change management, tool support, and strategy. We own way too much of the process. Would love for our role to be more as a consultant
  • Better connection to other P&C actions. For example, embedding inclusion verbiage and rewards language to performance comms and FAQs. Helping us show up as 1 P&C
  • A 'phone a friend' for questions instead of a form or asking Circuit
In Progress

Clearer, More Practical Communications & Guidance

Requests for simpler, more direct, and actionable guidance — less jargon-heavy and lengthy content.

Expected Timing

Q4FY26: Workday, Leader Actions, and Performance bot content will be simplified. Q2FY27: As we evolve Performance, we are evaluating the names of the Performance actions and fully rewriting the Performance narrative in plain, straightforward language.

  • Extremely crisp instructions/support on how to complete and monitor your data vs lengthy, multiple sources that can lean 'aspirational' or hr-coded rather than need-to-know/practical/user friendly
  • More concise comms to Leaders (including potentially a one pager cheat sheet?). They often come to Peeps directly for questions first because guides are lengthy
  • Clearer comms to Leaders
  • Clarity on comms being sent to Leaders, like NME removals and MYA exceptions
  • There is an opportunity to figure out the change management, comms, etc. in a more simple way. This is where there is often churn.

Tooling, Automation & Reporting

Requests to reduce manual work through automation, better dashboards, and improved tooling for talent review and status reporting.

Expected Timing

Q4FY26: Leaders can ask CIRCUIT for completion status of PDRs and TAs. FY27: As we evolve Performance, we will deliver connected data, advanced performance insights, and AI-enabled capabilities.

  • Automated reports or reminders with % completion to at least director plus level would be helpful
  • Talent Review digitization and tooling
  • Automate the status reports so they stop asking me to pull updates of who submitted or make it really clear where dashboards are
  • Moving talent assessments post manager change was impossible - the support teams wouldn't help make exceptions
  • Access to spot check whether the reviews were impactful
  • It feels like a check box exercise and in some ways Circuit makes it worse. Lots of words and the experience is not great for the team members
Future Step

Stronger Team & Org Talent Planning and Rewards Connection

Better visibility into talent planning, assessment cycles, and org changes — with more advance notice and end-to-end context.

Rationale

Once we evolve the Check-In experience, our next step is to revisit the Calibration module.

  • Visibility into team and org talent planning. Any changes?
  • Don't have a separate talent assessment module from org/team talent planning
  • Having the talent assessment and focal be closer together so that the assessment cycle doesn't feel like it is always on
  • A use case scenario end to end (starting from reflection all the way to TR and team and org planning)
  • Understanding on if we are shifting to measuring / rewarding potential or performance?
